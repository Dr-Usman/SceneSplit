## Information We Collect

SceneSplit is designed to work **without creating an account**. We do **not** collect your email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or payment information through the app.

The following information is stored **locally on your device** when you use the app:

- **Your display name** — the name you choose during setup or in your profile
- **People you add** — names of individuals you include in groups
- **Group details** — group names, emojis, and currency settings
- **Financial records you enter** — expenses, split amounts, percentages, balances, and settlement records

This information is entered by you and is used only to provide expense-splitting features within the app.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

SceneSplit does **not**:

- Require sign-up or login
- Upload your expense data to our servers
- Access your device contacts, camera, microphone, or location
- Run advertising or behavioral analytics
- Sell, rent, or trade your personal information

If you contact us by email (see [Contact Us](#contact-us)), we receive only the information you choose to include in your message.

---

## How We Use Your Information

Information stored on your device is used solely to:

- Create and manage groups and members
- Record and calculate shared expenses and balances
- Suggest and record settlements between members
- Display summaries and charts within the app

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the app’s core features.

---

## Data Storage and Security

- All app data is stored in a **local database on your device** using SQLite (via the Drift package).
- We do **not** operate cloud sync or remote backup services for your expense data.
- SceneSplit v1 does **not** include third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.

**Your responsibility:** Because data stays on your device, you are responsible for securing your phone or tablet (passcode, biometrics, device encryption) and for any backups created by your operating system (e.g. iCloud or Google backup). Uninstalling the app removes SceneSplit’s local data from your device.

## Third-Party Services

SceneSplit uses a small number of standard libraries that may interact with your device or, in limited cases, the internet:

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google Fonts** | App typography | May download font files from Google servers on first use; fonts are cached locally afterward |
| **In-app review** | Optional “Rate SceneSplit” prompt | Opens your platform’s native store review dialog; no expense data is sent |
| **Email client** | Contact us, feedback, feature suggestions | Opens your email app; we only receive what you send us |

These services are governed by their own privacy policies. SceneSplit does not pass your expense or group data to them.

## Device Permissions

SceneSplit requests **no special permissions** for storage, contacts, location, camera, or microphone on Android or iOS for normal use.

On some Android builds, an **INTERNET** permission may be present for development or font loading; the app does not transmit your expense data over the internet.

## Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your data at any time from within the app:

- Delete individual expenses, settlements, groups, or people
- Remove all data by **uninstalling SceneSplit** from your device

We do not retain copies of your locally stored data on our servers because we never receive it.

## Children’s Privacy

SceneSplit is not directed at children under **13** years of age (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will assist with removal on the device.

---

## International Users

SceneSplit stores data on your device in the country where you use the app. Because we do not collect or process your expense data on our servers, cross-border data transfers by SceneSplit do not apply to your in-app records.
