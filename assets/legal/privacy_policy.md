# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2026

SceneSplit ("we", "our", or "the app") is an offline expense-splitting app. This policy explains how your information is handled.

## Information we collect

SceneSplit stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- Your display name
- Names of people you add to groups
- Group names, emojis, and currency settings
- Expenses, splits, and settlement records you enter

We do **not** require an account, and we do **not** collect your email, phone number, or location.

## How we use your data

All data is used solely to provide expense-splitting features within the app on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

## Data storage and security

- All data is stored in a local database on your device.
- We do not upload your data to our servers.
- SceneSplit v1 does not include cloud sync, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

You are responsible for securing your device (passcode, biometrics, backups).

## Data retention and deletion

You can delete groups, expenses, people, and settlements from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

## Children's privacy

SceneSplit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
